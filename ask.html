Ask me anything about Srila Prabhupada's life — a year, an event, a teaching. I'll answer once a RAG backend is connected. Until then, I'll show you exactly what I would have sent.
Connecting your RAG pipeline — this page calls one function, queryArchive(question), in js/ask.js. Point window.SITE_CONFIG.RAG_API_ENDPOINT in js/config.js at your backend's query endpoint, and it will start returning real answers instead of the placeholder message above. Keep your actual API keys and vector DB credentials server-side, read from .env — never in this frontend. See .env.example and README.md for the full setup.
